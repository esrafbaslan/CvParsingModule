# Product Strategy

## Product Vision

Introduce the first AI-powered capability into the Digital HR SuperApp ecosystem and establish a foundation for future AI-assisted HR workflows.

The vision was not simply to automate CV processing. The goal was to transform candidate discovery from a manual, document-centric activity into an intelligent and scalable decision-support capability.

As the primary target market consisted of large enterprises in Türkiye, the solution needed to support high-volume recruitment operations while maintaining performance, reliability, security, and cost efficiency.

---

## Strategic Objectives

### Accelerate Recruitment Operations

Reduce the time recruiters spend reviewing, interpreting, and comparing candidate CVs by automatically extracting and structuring candidate information.

### Improve Candidate Discovery

Enable recruiters to identify qualified candidates based on skills, experience, competencies, and contextual relevance rather than relying solely on keyword matching.

### Reduce Dependency on Domain Expertise

Support recruitment processes requiring highly specialized technical or business knowledge.

Candidates often describe similar skills using different acronyms, technologies, certifications, or job titles. The platform should help recruiters identify relevant candidates even when they lack deep domain expertise.

### Build for Enterprise Scale

Support large recruitment volumes where hundreds or thousands of CVs may need to be processed, searched, and evaluated efficiently.

Scalability was considered a key product requirement from the beginning due to the target customer profile.

### Establish an AI Foundation for the HR Platform

Use the CV Parser initiative as the first production-grade AI capability within the Digital HR SuperApp roadmap.

The project should create reusable patterns and organizational knowledge around:

- LLM integration
- AI observability
- Evaluation frameworks
- AI security
- Cost management
- Model lifecycle management

These capabilities would accelerate future AI initiatives across the broader HR platform.

---

## Product Principles

### Structured Data First

CVs are unstructured documents.

The platform converts them into structured candidate profiles that can be searched, filtered, analyzed, ranked, and integrated into downstream HR workflows.

### Semantic Understanding Over Keywords

Traditional ATS solutions rely heavily on keyword matching.

The platform uses semantic search and AI-assisted matching to identify relevant candidates based on meaning rather than exact wording.

### Human-in-the-Loop

The system augments recruiter capabilities rather than replacing recruiter judgment.

Final hiring decisions remain with human stakeholders.

### Responsible AI Adoption

AI capabilities should deliver measurable business value while maintaining acceptable levels of cost, performance, explainability, and operational reliability.

Technology decisions should be continuously evaluated against both business outcomes and operating costs.

---

## Success Criteria

The product was expected to:

- Reduce CV review effort
- Improve candidate discovery
- Support large candidate volumes
- Provide a scalable foundation for AI-assisted recruitment workflows
- Establish the first AI-powered capability within the Digital HR SuperApp ecosystem
- Create reusable AI architecture, evaluation, and observability patterns for future HR products
