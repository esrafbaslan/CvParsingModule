# AI CV Parser 

A case study describing how I led product decisions for an AI-powered CV parsing and candidate matching platform.

## Business Problem

Recruiters spend significant time manually reviewing CVs.

The objective was to:

- Structure CVs automatically
- Support multilingual resumes
- Enable semantic candidate matching
- Reduce recruiter screening effort

## My Role

Product Lead

Responsibilities:

- Product strategy
- AI workflow design
- Architecture tradeoff decisions
- Cost optimization
- AI provider evaluation
- Security requirements
- Product roadmap

## Key Outcomes

- Reduced processing time from 45-60s to 10-15s
- Multi-provider AI architecture
- Semantic candidate matching
- Prompt injection protection
- Production observability
